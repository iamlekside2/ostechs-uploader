Privacy Policy — OS Techs Uploader

Last updated: 8 October 2026

OS Techs Uploader is a personal command-line tool used by the owner of the OS Techs YouTube channel to upload their own videos to their own channel via the YouTube Data API.

  • The tool is used only by its owner. It has no other users.
  • It does not collect, store, or share any personal data from anyone.
  • The Google OAuth token it uses is stored locally on the owner's computer and is never transmitted anywhere except to Google's own APIs.
  • It uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload videos, set thumbnails, and schedule publishing on the owner's channel.

Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact: the OS Techs channel owner via the email listed on the channel's about page.
